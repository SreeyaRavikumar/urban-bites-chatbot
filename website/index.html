Our Story

Welcome to Urban Bites, where the vibrant flavors of Indian street food come alive in a cozy, homely atmosphere. Our journey began with a simple passion for authentic recipes passed down through generations.

Every dish at Urban Bites is crafted with love and traditional spices, bringing the bustling street food culture of India right to your table. We believe in preserving authenticity while creating a comfortable dining experience.

Our chefs have mastered the art of blending spices to create that perfect balance of flavors that will transport you straight to the streets of Mumbai, Delhi, and beyond. Come join us for a culinary adventure!
